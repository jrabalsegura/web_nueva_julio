HVAC · Efficient ventilation

Heat recovery for more efficient air renewal

A mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery extracts stale air and supplies filtered outdoor air. During the exchange, it recovers part of the energy from the exhaust air, reducing the losses associated with continuous ventilation.

  • Central heat-recovery unit.
  • Insulated supply and extract ducts.
  • Protected, separate outdoor terminals.
  • Accessible design for adjustment and maintenance.
