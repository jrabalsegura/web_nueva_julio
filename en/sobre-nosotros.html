Who we are

Specialist engineering and personal service to deliver the best solution for every client

Resolution Solar Energy was created to bring well-designed technical solutions to clients who want to save energy and improve their installations.

Company

Resolution Solar Energy: technical expertise with a personal approach

Our work focuses on renewable energy, energy efficiency, climate control, electrical systems, drone inspections and maintenance. The aim is to avoid isolated measures and design installations that make sense in terms of consumption, use and future maintenance.

Our method is simple: listen, assess, propose, deliver and maintain. This way, every client understands what will be done, why it is recommended and how it can improve consumption, comfort or safety.

Our approach

  • Engineering applied to energy savings and comfort.
  • Reliable, orderly installations designed for straightforward inspections.
  • Solutions tailored to homes, residential communities, businesses and industry.
  • Clear communication before, during and after delivery.
